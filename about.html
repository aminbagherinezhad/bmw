Our story

Family-run care for your car, for over 40 years.

Sutton Car Care Centre is a DVSA-approved garage in Sutton Coldfield — trusted locally for MOT, servicing and repairs.

Who we are

A West Midlands garage built on trust.

We are a family-run business based on Coleshill Road / Norris Way corner in Sutton Coldfield. For more than four decades we have helped drivers keep their vehicles safe, reliable and road-legal.

From same-day MOT testing to full servicing, tyres, diagnostics and air conditioning, our experienced technicians look after cars, vans and more — with clear advice and no surprises.
